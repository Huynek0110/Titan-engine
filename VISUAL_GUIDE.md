## ?? FFmpeg Scale Formula

### BEFORE (Problematic)
```
FORMULA: scale2ref=w=iw*X:h=-1:flags=lanczos

Logo: 200×100 (width:height = 2:1)
Video: 1920×1080 (16:9)
Scale: 0.2

Result:
? On 16:9 video:   ? Works (usually)
? On 9:16 video:   ? Distorted!
? On 1:1 video:    ? Wrong ratio!

Issue: scale2ref can have issues with
       vertical videos and calculations
```

### AFTER (Correct)
```
FORMULA: scale=w=iw*X:h=-1:flags=lanczos

Logo: 200×100 (width:height = 2:1)
Scale: 0.2

Calculation:
w = input_width * scale = 200 * 0.2 = 40px
h = -1 (FFmpeg auto-calc) = 40 / 2.0 = 20px
Result: 40×20 (still 2:1 ratio ?)

Result:
? On 16:9 video:   ? Perfect (40×20)
? On 9:16 video:   ? Perfect (40×20)
? On 1:1 video:    ? Perfect (40×20)
? Logo never distorted!

Benefit: Works on ANY video aspect ratio
         Height auto-calculated from width
         Aspect ratio always preserved
```

### Mathematical Proof
```
Original Aspect Ratio:  AR = W/H = 200/100 = 2.0

Scaled with h=-1:
New Width:  w = 200 × 0.2 = 40px
New Height: h = 40 / 2.0 = 20px
New Ratio:  AR = 40/20 = 2.0 ?

PROVEN: h=-1 preserves original aspect ratio!
```
